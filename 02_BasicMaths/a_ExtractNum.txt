================================================================================
QUESTION: Extract Digits of a Number & Count Digits
FILE: a_ExtractNum.java
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS THIS PROBLEM ABOUT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Extract digits of a number one by one from right to left, and compute the total number of digits in the number.

2. HOW TO THINK (INTUITION)?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How do you get the last digit of any number in base 10?
- Take modulus 10: num % 10.
How do you chop off the last digit?
- Divide by 10: num / 10.
How do you count digits without a loop?
- Math formula: (int)Math.log10(num) + 1!

3. STEP-BY-STEP APPROACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extraction loop:
   - while (num > 0):
     - lastDigit = num % 10.
     - process/print lastDigit.
     - num = num / 10.
2. Fast Digit Counting formula:
   - count = (int) Math.log10(num) + 1.

4. WHY THIS WORKS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In base 10 arithmetic, dividing by 10 shifts all digits right by 1 position (discarding the remainder). Math.log10(x) tells you what power of 10 equals x; adding 1 gives the exact number of digits.

5. WHY NOT OTHER APPROACHES (WHY NOT THAT)?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why not convert to String: String.valueOf(num).length()?
Converting to string creates a new String object and character array in heap memory (O(N) space and slower). Modulus/division or log10 is O(1) space and super fast.

6. STEP-BY-STEP DRY RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Input: num = 1998
Formula: log10(1998) = 3.30059... -> (int)3.30059 = 3 -> 3 + 1 = 4 digits!
Loop extraction:
1998 % 10 = 8 -> num becomes 199
199 % 10 = 9 -> num becomes 19
19 % 10 = 9 -> num becomes 1
1 % 10 = 1 -> num becomes 0 -> Loop terminates.

7. COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Time Complexity: O(log10(N))
  Reason: A number N has roughly log10(N) digits. We divide by 10 each step.
- Space Complexity: O(1)
  Reason: No extra storage used.

8. IMPORTANT TIPS, TRAPS & EDGE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Notice that extracting with % 10 gives digits in REVERSE order (from right to left: 8, 9, 9, 1).
- Special case: num = 0. Math.log10(0) is negative infinity! For 0, count is manually 1.
