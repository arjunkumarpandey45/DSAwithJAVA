================================================================================
QUESTION: Tower of Hanoi
FILE: TowerOfHanoi.java
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS THIS PROBLEM ABOUT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Move N disks from a source rod to a destination rod using an auxiliary (helper) rod, such that:
1. Only one disk can be moved at a time.
2. A larger disk can never be placed on top of a smaller disk.

2. HOW TO THINK (INTUITION)?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Think recursively for N disks:
1. Move the top (N - 1) disks from Source to Helper (using Destination as temp).
2. Move the 1 remaining largest disk directly from Source to Destination.
3. Move the (N - 1) disks from Helper to Destination (using Source as temp)!
That's the entire problem!

3. STEP-BY-STEP APPROACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
void hanoi(int n, char src, char helper, char dest):
1. Base case: if (n == 0) return;
2. Step 1: hanoi(n - 1, src, dest, helper);
3. Step 2: System.out.println(src + " -> " + dest);
4. Step 3: hanoi(n - 1, helper, src, dest);

4. WHY THIS WORKS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By induction: if you can move N-1 disks anywhere safely, you can move the largest disk to destination, and then safely move the N-1 disks on top of it.

5. WHY NOT OTHER APPROACHES (WHY NOT THAT)?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why not solve iteratively?
Iterative Tower of Hanoi requires complex bitwise operations or explicit stacks. The recursive solution is 4 lines and mathematically elegant.

6. STEP-BY-STEP DRY RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For n = 2, src='A', helper='B', dest='C':
1. hanoi(1, A, C, B) -> prints "A -> B"
2. prints "A -> C" (largest disk moved)
3. hanoi(1, B, A, C) -> prints "B -> C"
Total moves = 2^2 - 1 = 3 moves!

7. COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Time Complexity: O(2^N)
  Reason: Total moves required is exactly 2^N - 1.
- Space Complexity: O(N)
  Reason: Maximum recursion stack depth is N.

8. IMPORTANT TIPS, TRAPS & EDGE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Notice how the parameters swap roles: in step 1 dest acts as the helper; in step 3 src acts as the helper!
